### 🛠️ Code of Conduct & Guidelines

* **Transparency First**: As stated in our README, this software must remain 100% free of adware, bloatware, tracking scripts, hidden telemetry, or misleading advertising. Any contribution attempting to introduce tracking or third-party analytical SDKs will be immediately rejected and blocked.
* **Kotlin & Compose Standards**: Please follow the official JetBrains/Kotlin formatting guidelines. Ensure that all SQLDelight schema changes are properly versioned and do not corrupt the local-only execution environment.
* **Privacy-First Mindset**: Do not introduce any code that relies on external cloud synchronization. The application must remain strictly 100% offline.
